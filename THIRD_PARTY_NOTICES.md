# Third-party dependencies

The contract imports `ERC20` from OpenZeppelin Contracts 5.6.1. That package
is distributed under the MIT license and identifies its author as the
OpenZeppelin Community. Its source and notices remain in the installed package.

[OpenZeppelin Contracts upstream repository](https://github.com/OpenZeppelin/openzeppelin-contracts)

The build and test tools include Hardhat, the Hardhat ethers plugin, ethers and
solc, along with their transitive dependencies. Their versions, source package
URLs, integrity hashes and package license metadata are recorded in
`package-lock.json`. Each dependency retains its own license and notices.

Dependencies are not vendored or relicensed by this repository. Installing them
with npm downloads the packages and their accompanying notices. The root MIT
license applies to project-authored files only.
